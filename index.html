Attribution

See What Your Marketing Actually Generates.

Ad platforms count clicks and form fills. Your CRM knows who booked, showed up, and bought. SyncTada connects the two, so you can follow spend all the way to qualified leads, customers, revenue, and ROAS.

  • Spend → leads → qualified → booked → sales → revenue
  • Phone calls tied back to the ad or listing that drove them
  • Honest labels: attributed revenue, not guesses about causation

Optimization

Your Ads Could Be Working Smarter.

Most marketers don't know some of these are possible. Here are a few things better data makes possible.

CRM: Sold→Meta / Google

What if Meta knew which leads actually bought?

Send qualified leads, appointments, and sales back to supported ad platforms, so their algorithms can find more people like your real customers, not just cheap form fills.

CRM conversion feedback · Conversions API
Ad #3→Tracking number→Call

Which ads made your phone ring?

Dedicated tracking numbers connect phone calls to the campaign or listing that drove them, so calls count toward results instead of disappearing.

Call tracking & attribution
Acquisition ad→Existing customer

Why advertise to people who already bought?

Exclude existing customers and already-booked leads from acquisition ads where it makes sense, and put that budget toward new prospects.

Audience exclusions
Audience AAudience BWinner

Could a different audience or creative perform better?

Use your first-party data to build custom audiences, then test audiences, visuals, and copy against qualified leads and sales, not just clicks.
