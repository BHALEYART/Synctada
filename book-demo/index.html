Demo FAQ

How long is the demo?

We'll confirm the time when we schedule. Demos are tailored to your setup, so we keep them focused on what's relevant to you.

Is the audit free?

Yes. After the demo, eligible prospects receive a free attribution and optimization audit, plus a recommendations report and three-month playbook. Implementation is a separate paid engagement.

What systems do you support?

We're focused on GoHighLevel and HubSpot CRMs and Meta and Google ad platforms. Tell us your stack in the form and we'll confirm what's available for you.

Can my agency or marketer attend?

Absolutely. Business owners often bring their agency or marketer, and agencies sometimes bring a client. We work best as a partner to your existing team.

Is this a fit for my business?

SyncTada fits best when ads generate leads, calls, or appointments, and a sales process turns some into customers. Not sure? Book the demo and we'll tell you honestly.
