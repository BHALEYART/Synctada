The process, step by step

  1. 01

    Step 1 · Personalized demo

    We learn how your marketing works.

    We talk through your business, services, lead-to-sale process, CRM, ad platforms, reporting, and goals. Then we show how the software and hands-on service work together, and agree on the scope of a follow-up audit if you're eligible.

  2. 02

    Step 2 · Free audit

    A free attribution & optimization audit.

    We review your current setup, as applicable:

    • Campaign and source tracking, including UTM consistency
    • CRM lead stages, appointment outcomes, sales and revenue data
    • Website tracking, pixels, server-side events, and conversion API integrations
    • Call tracking and attribution
    • Ad-platform conversion goals, and whether quality or sales signals are sent back
    • Audience exclusions, retargeting, and custom audiences
    • Creative, copy, placement, and schedule testing opportunities
    • Client-facing reporting and ROAS visibility

    Depth depends on the access and data available. We ask only for the permissions we need and avoid unnecessary sensitive customer data.

  3. 03

    Step 3 · Recommendations + playbook

    A prioritized report and three-month playbook.

    Executive summary. Where measurement and optimization stand today.
    Findings. What's working, what's missing, what's uncertain.
    Opportunity register. Each idea with potential impact, effort, dependencies, and priority.
    Measurement plan. Events, attribution sources, KPIs, and reporting needs.
    Three-month playbook. Sequenced actions, owners, and success criteria.
    Next steps. Proposed implementation scope and how we'd work together.

    Impact estimates are directional, not promises.

  4. 04

    Step 4 · Implementation

    Put the playbook to work.

    As a customer, SyncTada implements the plan with you. Implementation and ongoing services are a separate paid engagement, scoped to your needs.

The three-month playbook

Measure. Automate. Test.

A recommended sequence, adapted to every account. Some work overlaps; you don't have to wait for perfect attribution to start optimizing.

Month 1

Measure

Build a trustworthy link between marketing and business outcomes.

  • Connect CRM and ad platforms
  • Audit and repair UTMs, pixels, forms, and source capture
  • Configure server-side tracking and supported conversion APIs
  • Map CRM milestones: qualified, booked, attended, sold, revenue
  • Set up call tracking where relevant
  • Initial dashboards and baseline KPIs
DeliverableWorking measurement foundation, baseline view, and tracking QA findings.
Month 2

Automated optimization

Give existing campaigns better signals and cut obvious waste.

  • Send eligible CRM events and values to supported platforms
  • Validate matching, deduplication, consent, and signal volume
  • Configure optimization events and goals
  • Build and test exclusion audiences
  • Review retargeting, lead quality, and budget allocation
DeliverableValidated conversion feedback, recommended changes, and an early optimization scorecard.
Month 3

Manual tests

Test human-directed improvements informed by attribution data.

  • Ad visuals: imagery, video, formats, hooks
  • Ad copy: messaging, offers, headlines, CTAs
  • Custom audiences, including permitted external segments
  • Placements, scheduling, geography, and budget strategy
  • Documented hypotheses, measured on lead quality and sales
DeliverableTest results, recommendations, and a next-quarter plan.
"Automated optimization" means ad-platform optimization systems powered by better data. Audience and data use always follow platform policies and privacy requirements; regulated industries like finance, insurance, housing, and legal may have additional restrictions.

After month three
