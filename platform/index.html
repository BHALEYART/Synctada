03 · Automated optimization & insights

Improve What Isn't Working.

Ad platforms optimize toward whatever you tell them counts. When they only see form fills, they find more form fills. Feed back qualified leads, appointments, and sales, and they can learn what a real customer looks like.

  • Downstream CRM outcomes sent to supported ad platforms
  • Audience exclusions for existing customers and already-booked leads, where appropriate
  • Wasted spend, lead-quality differences, and funnel bottlenecks surfaced
  • Recommendations you approve, not unannounced changes to your campaigns

Common questions

Platform FAQ

Is attribution 100% accurate?

No attribution system is. Some visitors browse across devices, decline tracking, or come back later through another channel. SyncTada improves how much you can connect and is upfront about what it can't see.

What's the difference between attributed and incremental revenue?

Attributed revenue is revenue from customers whose journey we can connect to a campaign. Incremental revenue is revenue that wouldn't have happened without the ad, which requires controlled testing to measure. We label the two separately.

What is the Conversions API (CAPI)?

It's a way to send conversion events to Meta directly from a server instead of relying only on a browser pixel. It helps with reliability and lets you send CRM outcomes, like a booked appointment, that happen after someone leaves your website. It isn't a workaround for missing consent.

Does SyncTada change my campaigns automatically?

The automated part is sending better conversion signals to supported ad platforms. Changes to audiences, creative, copy, or budgets are recommended and approved by you or your team.
